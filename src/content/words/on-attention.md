---
title: On attention, and two bunnies
date: 2026-06-27
summary: What living with prey animals taught me about patience.
lang: en
---

Bunnies don't perform affection. They won't run to the door when you get home, and
they won't pretend to like you because you feed them. Trust is earned in millimeters.

There's something clarifying about that. You learn to slow down, to make yourself
small and predictable, to let another creature set the pace. You stop optimizing.

I think good work is a little like this too — you show up quietly, again and again,
and one day the thing you were coaxing simply leans in and takes the banana.
