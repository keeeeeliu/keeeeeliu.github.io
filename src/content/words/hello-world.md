For a long time this was a page with my name and a list of links. Useful, forgettable.

I wanted something closer to a room — a place with my two bunnies in it, some music
playing if you want it, a few things I've made, and the occasional thought I couldn't
shake. Less a portfolio, more a home you can wander through.

So: welcome. Take your shoes off. Pet the bunny in the corner (click it — it hops).
